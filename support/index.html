Please don't send personal health details by email unless you want us to see them. A screenshot of the screen that went wrong is usually enough.

Common questions

A reminder didn't come. Check iPhone Settings › Notifications › Steady is allowed, and in Steady open the medication and check that today's day is switched on in Schedule. Focus modes can also hold notifications.

How do I log a dose I forgot? History › + › choose the medication and the date and time.

How do I cancel Steady Pro? iPhone Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions › Steady Pro › Cancel Subscription. Your log stays and logging remains free.

I changed phones. Where is my data? With Steady Pro, your log syncs through your own iCloud. Sign in to the same Apple Account on the new iPhone and open Steady. Without Pro, data stays on the old iPhone.

How do I delete everything? Steady › Settings › Delete all data. This removes your data from the iPhone and from your iCloud.

Can Steady tell me if my dose is right? No. Steady records what your prescriber set and what you log. For anything about your treatment, ask your prescriber.
