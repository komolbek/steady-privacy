Steady Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 October 2026

Developer: Komol Ibragimov ("we", "us")

Contact: kamol.developer@gmail.com

Steady is a log for people on hormone replacement therapy (HRT). It helps you record the doses and patch changes your prescriber set, track symptoms, and create a summary for your appointments.

The short version: Steady has no account and no servers of ours. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone and, if you turn on sync, in your own private iCloud. We cannot see, access, sell or share your data. Steady contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

2. Apple Health (HealthKit)

If you choose Import from Apple Health, Steady asks for permission to read two types of data: sleep analysis and sleeping wrist temperature. Steady uses them only to show them next to your symptoms inside the app.

  • Steady never writes to Apple Health.
  • Data read from Apple Health is stored on your iPhone only. It is not synced to iCloud and never leaves your device, unless you include it in a PDF that you choose to share.
  • It is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, and never shared with third parties, including data brokers.
  • You can turn off access at any time in the iPhone Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › Steady.

3. iCloud sync (Steady Pro)

If you have Steady Pro and are signed in to iCloud, Steady syncs your health log between your devices using Apple's CloudKit private database. This data is stored in your own iCloud account. Apple's Privacy Policy and iCloud terms apply. As the developer, we have no access to your private iCloud database.

Without Steady Pro, or if you are not signed in to iCloud, nothing is synced: your data stays on the iPhone.

4. Reminders

Dose reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. They are not sent through our servers or any third party. You can turn them off in Settings › Notifications › Steady.

5. PDF summary

When you create a PDF summary, it is generated on your iPhone. It leaves the device only if you choose to share or save it, for example by email or AirDrop to your prescriber. After that, the recipient's or the service's own privacy practices apply.

6. Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your name, payment card or billing details. Steady checks your subscription status with Apple on your device. To manage or cancel your subscription: Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions.

7. Diagnostics

Steady has no crash-reporting or analytics tools of its own. If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone settings (Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements), Apple may share anonymous crash reports and usage statistics with us. These do not contain your health log.

8. Not medical advice

Steady records what you enter. It does not give medical advice, does not recommend or judge doses, and does not diagnose. Always follow your prescriber's instructions.

9. How long data is kept and how to delete it

Your data stays until you delete it.

  • Single entries: edit or delete any dose, symptom or medication in the app.
  • Everything at once: in Steady, go to Settings › Delete all data and confirm. This permanently deletes, from your iPhone and from your iCloud, all medications, schedules, the dose log, symptoms, patch sites, period dates and data imported from Apple Health. It also removes all scheduled reminders. Other devices signed in to the same iCloud account remove the data when they next sync. If one of them was offline for a long time, open Steady on it and use Delete all data there too. To delete the iCloud copy, your iPhone must be online and signed in to iCloud. If it isn't, Steady deletes the data on the iPhone and tells you that the iCloud copy remains.
  • Deleting the app: removes everything stored on the iPhone, but not the copy in your iCloud. To remove that too, use Delete all data first, or go to iPhone Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Account Storage › Steady › Delete Data.
  • Not affected: your Steady Pro subscription (manage it in Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions) and data in Apple Health, which belongs to the Health app.

Because we hold no copy of your data, we cannot restore it if you delete it, and we have nothing to delete or export on our side.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, or to object to its processing. This includes the UK GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), and Washington's My Health My Data Act. Because all data stays on your device and in your own iCloud, you can exercise these rights directly in the app and in your iPhone settings, as described above. We do not sell or share personal data, consumer health data included, and we do not use it for targeted advertising. If you have a question or a request, contact us at kamol.developer@gmail.com. We will reply within 30 days.

UK users can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

11. Children

Steady is intended for adults. It is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above. If a change affects how data is handled, for example if we ever add a feature that sends data off your device, we will describe it in the app before it takes effect.
